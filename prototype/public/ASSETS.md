## AI-edited placement example

`images/van-dublin-ad.webp` is an AI-edited derivative of Mathias Reding’s photograph above, created with OpenAI’s built-in image-generation tool. It illustrates a possible vinyl placement, not an actual installation. The “See the blank canvas” control shows the untouched `van-dublin.webp` source image.

Edit prompt: Preserve the original van, street, camera angle, building, lighting and photographic texture. Apply a signal-yellow vinyl advertising decal inside the recessed cargo panel with dark petrol condensed lettering reading “GOOD THINGS” and “GET AROUND.” plus a diagonal arrow. Follow the panel perspective, curvature and central door seam so the decal looks physically installed. Add no interface graphics or other scene changes.


## Motorcycle pilot artwork

The downloadable Everywhere motorcycle design is original vector artwork using the project’s colors and wordmark. Lettering is outlined from Bricolage Grotesque (Bricolage Grotesque Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1). It still uses the earlier pink/cobalt folded-poster identity and has not been redrawn in the Kinetic identity yet. It is a draft composition with no physical print size; the permitted panel must be measured and the printer must approve its fit before production.

## Fonts

The production identity uses self-hosted **Roboto Flex** for display and interface text. The browser makes no Google Fonts request.

- `fonts/roboto-flex-latin-variable.woff2` — The Roboto Flex Project Authors, copyright 2017. Latin subset with weight (300–1000), width (100–151) and slant (−10–0) axes; the parametric axes are fixed at their defaults and optical size at 32. [Original project](https://github.com/TypeNetwork/Roboto-Flex), [Google Fonts source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/robotoflex), [included SIL Open Font License 1.1](fonts/roboto-flex-OFL.txt).

## Brand mark

The Kinetic wordmark and app mark are original vector artwork. Their letterforms are outlined from Roboto Flex (width 151, weight 900, slant −8) under the same licence; the trail and speed lines are drawn shapes. The homepage scroll story is authored SVG; Juniper Coffee and Northside Bikes in it are fictional advertisers and its offer-card prices are example values.


## Generated team-test vehicles

The four JPEGs in `images/team-tests/` were generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 12 November 2026, then converted to JPEG for listing covers. They depict fictional reference vehicles: a red sport motorcycle, blue hatchback, white van and green cargo bicycle. These images are used in labelled team-run mainnet test campaigns. They do not establish vehicle ownership, location, availability or physical installation. The [prompt manifest](images/team-tests/prompts.json) contains the complete prompt for each image.

## Homepage placement illustration

The homepage uses `images/setup-van.webp`, a compressed WebP copy of `images/team-tests/van.jpg`, the generated reference described above. Two illustrative SVG contours match that photo. They reveal automatically once in two seconds, then remain still; reduced-motion and data-saving visitors see the completed illustration immediately. This presentation makes no AI request, creates no listing, and requests no wallet signature or transaction. Actual analysis and editing remain in owner setup.
