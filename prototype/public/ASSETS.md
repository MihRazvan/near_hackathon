## AI-edited placement example

`images/van-dublin-ad.webp` is an AI-edited derivative of Mathias Reding’s photograph above, created with OpenAI’s built-in image-generation tool. It illustrates a possible vinyl placement, not an actual installation. The “See the blank canvas” control shows the untouched `van-dublin.webp` source image.

Edit prompt: Preserve the original van, street, camera angle, building, lighting and photographic texture. Apply a signal-yellow vinyl advertising decal inside the recessed cargo panel with dark petrol condensed lettering reading “GOOD THINGS” and “GET AROUND.” plus a diagonal arrow. Follow the panel perspective, curvature and central door seam so the decal looks physically installed. Add no interface graphics or other scene changes.

## Homepage campaign story

`images/setup-van.webp` is a compressed copy of the generated van reference above. `images/story-coffee.webp` and `images/story-everywhere.webp` were edited from that reference with OpenAI’s built-in image-generation tool on 14 November 2026. They show fictional Daybreak Coffee and Everywhere vinyl creatives applied to the same van. Their purpose is an illustrative reservation/takeover sequence, not evidence of a physical installation or actual advertiser booking.

The four animated chapters illustrate scan/prompt setup, placement editing, a reservation/takeover, and the final placement NFT after booking closes. The example sends no AI request, payment, wallet signature or blockchain transaction. Its NFT card is illustrative. Real campaign evidence is available separately in the public records. The sequence runs once, pauses offscreen or when requested, and presents its final still frame for reduced-motion/data-saving visitors.
