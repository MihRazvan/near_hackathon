From a vehicle to a campaign.

Six steps. Clear dates. One all-in placement price.

1. Scan or describe

The owner confirms panels, dates and prices, then publishes.

2. Reserve or take over

Advertisers pay USDC before booking closes.

3. Receive the NFT

The final payer gets the creative right after close.

4. Approve the artwork

The current holder submits. The owner locks a revision.

5. Install and run

The owner uploads evidence. Participants can raise issues.

6. Claim and withdraw

Settlement credits the original entitled wallets.

Printing and installation are included. Takeovers end before production.
