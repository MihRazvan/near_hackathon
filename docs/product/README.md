# Product definition and mock design handoff

Research date: **8 November 2026**. Status: **interactive mock implemented for design review**. Core decisions below are confirmed; remaining policy proposals are open. Production integration is separate work.

Confirmed decisions: fixed campaign dates with takeovers ending before installation; the space owner arranges printing/installation and uploads proof; one buyer campaign can purchase spaces across different owners. The experience map marks these separately from remaining proposals.

Also confirmed: instant booking under published rules followed by artwork review; wallet-only sign-in; per-spot takeover controls with a sale-wide default.

Pricing and settlement: the advertised spot price includes printing and installation. Owner campaign earnings become available after the campaign ends and its issue-review window closes. Owners cover production before that release; the exact review-window duration and dispute rules remain open.

The complete, navigable mock covers an advertising marketplace: people browse physical advertising inventory, buy placements, manage artwork and delivery, and list and operate their own assets. Vehicles are the initial inventory type; the product language is **advertise on everything**.
